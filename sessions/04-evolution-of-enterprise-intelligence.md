# Session 04 — The Evolution of Enterprise Intelligence & Decision DNA

## User Prompt & Strategic Direction
Refined the conceptual framing for judges, introducing the 4-Generation Progression and the definition of **Decision DNA**:

```text
The Evolution of Enterprise Intelligence
Generation 1: Enterprise Search ("What happened?")
Generation 2: Copilots & RAG ("What do the documents say?")
Generation 3: Bob Time Machine ("Why was the decision made?")
Generation 4: Organizational Intelligence ("What should we do next?")

The Missing Layer:
For decades, enterprises focused on storing information.
Search improved our ability to find information.
Copilots improved our ability to synthesize information.
Neither explains why decisions were made, which stakeholders influenced them, 
or whether their assumptions still hold today. That missing layer is organizational reasoning.

Decision DNA:
Decision DNA = Catalyst + Evidence + Stakeholders + Trade-offs + Approvals + Implementation + Outcomes
```
