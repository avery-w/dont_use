# Session 03 — Epistemic Rigor, Missing Context Auditing & Historical Replay

## User Prompt
- Evaluated the complete canonical dossier containing:
  - Executive Summary
  - Organizational Timeline
  - Alternatives Considered (OpenShift vs. Rancher vs. Legacy)
  - Key Decision Drivers
  - Evidence Lineage & Reasoning Linkage (91%)
  - Missing Context Analysis (-3% confidence discount on missing cost spreadsheet)
  - Historical Decision Replay as of 2025-04-10 (Zero hindsight bias)
  - Present-Day Re-Evaluation ("Still Recommended") and Future Drift Triggers.

## Key Insights Established
1. **Epistemic Accounting**: Exposing missing artifacts (e.g., deleted Slack comments, unarchived spreadsheets) and calculating a formal uncertainty penalty rather than pretending data is 100% complete.
2. **Historical Replay Isolation**: Simulating decisions strictly on pre-vote historical context without contamination from future post-mortems or hindsight bias.
3. **Trigger-Based Governance**: Defining proactive conditions (cloud strategy shifts, budget cuts) under which an approved ADR must be reopened.
